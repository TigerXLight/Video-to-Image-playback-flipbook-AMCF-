## Public claim boundaries

The public repository must continue to avoid claiming:

- physical proof
- hardware validation
- final UI
- manufactured prototype
- arbitrary real video ingestion
- MP4/video-container export
- real scanner operation
- real printing
- real physical playback

Optional deep-tool adapters must remain separate unless their licences and redistribution rules are explicitly handled.
